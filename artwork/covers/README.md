# Evently cover artwork

These three original 3D illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for Evently. The PNG files here are full-resolution masters. Optimized JPEG versions in `public/covers/` are served by the app.

All three used this base art direction: portrait 4:5 event artwork, premium editorial 3D render for a youthful European design and culture platform; acid lime `#d8ff45`, violet `#5638ef`, coral `#ff6846`, warm ivory `#f2ede3`, near black `#18171d`; bold composition readable at card size; tactile high-end 3D, dramatic lighting and crisp shadows; no text, logo, border, watermark or people.

1. **01-future-work.png** — glossy violet chrome orb floating through a lime architectural portal with translucent coral glass spheres; creative technology and future-of-work theme.
2. **02-after-dark.png** — coral glass sun above concentric violet metallic sound rings and a black circular stage; festival and music after dark.
3. **03-creative-play.png** — inflated lime sphere, folded ivory paper ribbons, coral glass torus and violet plinth in an open-air pavilion; design and creative community.
